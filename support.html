技术支持

有问题或建议，请发邮件到 saltkeyboard.app@gmail.com。报告问题时，请写上 iOS 版本、机型和复现步骤。

如何启用 Salt 键盘？

打开“设置”→“通用”→“键盘”→“键盘”→“添加新键盘”，选择 Salt。打字时长按或轻点地球键，就能切换到 Salt。

需要开启“完全访问”吗？

不需要。开启后才有按键震动和候选栏的 Salt 快捷入口，其他功能都不受影响。Salt 键盘不联网。

怎么切换简体和繁体？

在 Salt App 的设置里选择输出字形：简体、繁体，或台湾正体。

怎么导入其他 Rime 输入法的词库？

把 *.userdb.txt 文件放进“文件”App 中 Salt 文件夹的 Inbox/，打开 Salt App 时会自动导入并合并。

换手机后词库还在吗？

Salt 每天会在 Backups/ 里自动备份。可以把 Salt 文件夹里的备份拷到新设备，在 Salt App 里恢复。

Support

For questions or feedback, email saltkeyboard.app@gmail.com. When reporting a bug, please include your iOS version, device model and the steps to reproduce it.

How do I enable Salt?

Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Salt. While typing, tap or hold the globe key to switch to it.

Do I need Full Access?

No. Full Access only enables haptics and the candidate-bar shortcut to the Salt app. Everything else works without it, and the keyboard never goes online.

How do I switch between Simplified and Traditional?

Choose the output script in Salt's settings: Simplified, Traditional or Taiwan Standard.

Can I import dictionaries from other Rime apps?

Put *.userdb.txt files in the Inbox/ folder inside Salt's folder in the Files app. They are imported and merged the next time you open Salt.
