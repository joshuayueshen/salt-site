隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

你打的字，只属于你。Salt 的隐私保障来自架构设计，而不只是承诺。

我们不收集任何数据

Salt 不收集、不存储、不传输你的个人信息或输入内容。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也不追踪你。

键盘不联网

Salt 键盘扩展不发出任何网络请求。输入、候选、联想和学习都由 Rime 引擎在你的设备上完成。

“完全访问”的用途

iOS 对所有开启“完全访问”的键盘都会显示同一条提示。Salt 只用这个权限做两件事：按键震动，以及从候选栏打开 Salt App。关闭“完全访问”后，打字功能不受影响。

数据存放在哪里

  • 用户词库和设置保存在你设备上的 App Group 容器里，只有 Salt App 和 Salt 键盘能访问。
  • Salt 文件夹可以在“文件”App 中看到，里面有自动备份和导出文件，都在你的设备上。
  • 如果你开启了 iCloud，数据只会同步到你自己的 iCloud 账户，开发者无法访问。

可选下载

只有当你主动选择下载“雾凇拼音”词库时，Salt App 才会联网，从该项目在 GitHub 上的官方仓库下载。这个请求不包含你的任何个人信息，但 GitHub 作为服务方，可能按其隐私政策记录访问信息（例如 IP 地址）。

儿童隐私

Salt 不收集任何人的数据，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变更，会更新本页面和生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

What you type belongs to you. Salt protects your privacy by design, not just by promise.

We collect no data

Salt does not collect, store or transmit any personal information or anything you type. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

The keyboard never goes online

The Salt keyboard extension makes no network requests. Input, suggestions and learning all happen on your device through the Rime engine.

Full Access

iOS shows the same notice for every keyboard that asks for Full Access. Salt uses it only for haptic feedback and for a shortcut that opens the Salt app from the candidate bar. Typing works normally with Full Access turned off.

Where your data lives

  • Your user dictionary and settings stay in an App Group container on your device. Only the Salt app and the Salt keyboard can read it.
  • The Salt folder in the Files app holds backups and exports, all on your device.
  • If you turn on iCloud, data syncs only to your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access it.

Optional downloads

The Salt app goes online only when you choose to download the optional rime-ice dictionary, which comes from that project's official GitHub repository. The request carries none of your personal information. GitHub may log access details such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

Children

Salt collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
