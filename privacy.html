隐私政策

盐输入法（下称「本 App」）由个人开发者 Yue Shen 开发。本政策说明本 App 和它的键盘扩展如何处理你的数据。简单地说：你打的字只在你的设备上处理，开发者不收集任何数据。

1. 键盘不联网

  • 键盘扩展不发出任何网络请求。你输入的内容由设备上的 Rime 引擎处理，不会被发送、上传或收集。
  • 即使开启「完全访问」，键盘也不联网。
  • 在密码输入框中，iOS 会自动换用系统键盘，本 App 的键盘接触不到密码。

2. 保存在你设备上的数据

为了让输入越用越顺手，本 App 在你的设备上保存以下内容：

  • 用户词库：键盘学会的词和词频；
  • 你粘贴的主题词库、你导入的用户词库；
  • 不再自动更正的英文词、选过的 emoji（可在设置中关闭「记住我选的 emoji」）；
  • 输入设置，例如拼音方案、模糊音、字形、键盘布局；
  • 用户词库和设置的自动备份、导出文件，存放在「文件」App 里可以看到的盐输入法文件夹中。

这些数据只保存在本机：词库和设置在本 App 与键盘共用的 App 群组容器里，备份和导出在本 App 自己的文件夹里。删除本 App 会一并删除它们，你另存到别处的副本除外。

键盘提供英文拼写建议时，会使用 iOS 提供给键盘的通讯录姓名和「文本替换」。这些内容由系统交给键盘，只在设备上使用，不会保存到别处或发送出去。本 App 不请求通讯录权限。

3. App 什么时候联网

只有主 App 会联网，而且只在以下两种情况：

  1. 下载雾凇拼音词库。只有在你点「下载雾凇词库」或「检查更新」时，App 才会从雾凇拼音在 GitHub 上的官方仓库（github.com / raw.githubusercontent.com）下载词库文件。这只是下载，不会上传你的任何数据。和访问任何网站一样，GitHub 会收到常规的网络请求信息（如 IP 地址），适用 GitHub 的隐私政策。
  2. iCloud 同步。见下一节。

此外，当你点 App 内的项目主页或开源许可链接时，会在浏览器中打开对应网页。

4. iCloud 同步

  • iCloud 同步默认开启，可在「设置」›「iCloud 同步」中随时关闭。
  • 同步的内容：用户词库（学会的词）、主题词库、不再自动更正的英文词，以及以下输入设置：模糊音、拼音方案、字形、英文拼写建议、自动更正、拼音显示在输入框中、九宫格英文候选。
  • 这些数据通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 存在你自己 iCloud 账号的私有数据库里。开发者无法访问私有数据库，看不到你的数据。存储和传输由 Apple 负责，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。
  • 同步只由主 App 完成，键盘扩展不联网。

5. 我们不做的事

  • 没有账号，不需要登录。
  • 没有广告，没有第三方统计或分析工具，不追踪你。
  • 开发者不收集、不出售、不分享任何个人数据或输入内容。

6. 「完全访问」

「完全访问」不是必须的。本 App 只用它来做按键震动、在候选栏打开 App，以及让键盘把不再自动更正的英文词和选过的 emoji 存到与 App 共用的位置。它不会让键盘联网。

7. 儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人数据，包括儿童。

8. 未来的功能

当前版本没有 AI 功能。将来如果加入需要联网的功能（例如按你的要求生成 AI 词库），只会在你主动使用时由主 App 联网，键盘仍然不联网；上线前我们会先更新本政策。

9. 政策变更与联系方式

本政策如有变更，会在本页更新并修改生效日期。有任何问题，请发邮件到 saltkeyboard.app@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Salt (盐输入法 / 鹽輸入法, "the app") is made by independent developer Yue Shen. This policy explains how the app and its keyboard extension handle your data. In short: what you type is processed only on your device, and the developer collects no data.

1. The keyboard never goes online

  • The keyboard extension makes no network requests. What you type is processed on your device by the Rime engine and is never sent, uploaded or collected.
  • This stays true with Full Access turned on.
  • In password fields iOS switches to the system keyboard, so Salt's keyboard never sees passwords.

2. Data stored on your device

To get better as you type, the app stores the following on your device:

  • your user dictionary: words the keyboard has learned and how often you use them;
  • topic word lists you paste in, and user dictionaries you import;
  • English words you told it not to autocorrect, and emoji you pick (turn off 记住我选的 emoji in Settings to stop remembering emoji);
  • input settings such as pinyin scheme, fuzzy pinyin, script and keyboard layout;
  • automatic backups and exports of your user dictionary and settings, in the Salt folder you can see in the Files app.

All of this stays on your device: dictionaries and settings in an App Group container shared by the app and its keyboard, backups and exports in the app's own folder. Deleting the app deletes them, except copies you have saved elsewhere.

For English suggestions, the keyboard uses the contact names and Text Replacements that iOS provides to keyboards. The system hands these to the keyboard; they are used on device only and are never stored elsewhere or sent anywhere. The app does not ask for Contacts permission.

3. When the app goes online

Only the main app goes online, and only in two cases:

  1. Downloading the rime-ice dictionary. Only when you tap Download (下载雾凇词库) or Check for updates (检查更新) does the app download dictionary files from the official rime-ice repository on GitHub (github.com / raw.githubusercontent.com). This is a download only; none of your data is uploaded. As with visiting any website, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy policy.
  2. iCloud sync. See the next section.

Tapping a project homepage or open-source license link in the app opens that page in your browser.

4. iCloud sync

  • iCloud sync is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in 设置 (Settings) › iCloud 同步.
  • What syncs: your user dictionary (learned words), topic word lists, English words you told it not to autocorrect, and these input settings: fuzzy pinyin, pinyin scheme, script, English suggestions, autocorrect, pinyin shown in the text field, and 9-key English candidates.
  • This data is stored with Apple's CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account. The developer has no access to private databases and can't see your data. Apple stores and transfers it under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Only the main app syncs. The keyboard extension never goes online.

5. What we don't do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking.
  • The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal data or anything you type.

6. Full Access

Full Access is optional. The app uses it only for haptic feedback, the candidate-bar shortcut that opens the app, and letting the keyboard save English words you told it not to autocorrect, and the emoji you pick, where the app can see them. It does not let the keyboard go online.

7. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Future features

This version has no AI features. If we add a feature that needs the internet (for example, generating an AI dictionary when you ask for one), only the main app will go online, only when you use it, and the keyboard will still never go online. We will update this policy before such a feature ships.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email saltkeyboard.app@gmail.com.
